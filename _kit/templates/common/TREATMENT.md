# {{TITLE}}: Treatment

Write this before drawing anything. Keep it short; it is the plan the clips are checked against.

## 1. Brief
- Topic, audience, length, language(s), must-have moments, the user's own material (photos, voice, music).

## 2. Logline and arc
- One sentence. Then **setup → turn → ending** (one subject, one want, one change; the ending echoes the opening).
- **Hook** in the first 3 s. **One native move** of the style at the emotional peak (see STYLE.md §Story).

## 3. Benchmark
- 1–2 reference works. **Learn:** composition, pacing, colour logic, score structure. **Don't take:** characters,
  designs, melodies, specific shots, logos.

## 4. Clip list (the storyboard in words)
| # | id | s | framing / camera | what happens | caption / line | sound |
|---|----|---|------------------|--------------|----------------|-------|
| 0 | 00-title | 6 | wide, slow push | … | … | … |

## 5. Beat sheet + cue map
- Second by second for key clips: where each action, caption and cut lands; tempo/bar grid if the music leads.
- Hold rules: a caption ≥ 1.8 s and ≥ spoken line + 0.6 s; after text lands, hold ≈ chars ÷ 4.5 + 1.5 s (中文)
  or letters ÷ 15 + 1.5 s (English); a title card ≥ 4 s.

## 6. Sound design
| section | ambience | foley (on actions) | music state | silence? |
|---------|----------|--------------------|-------------|----------|
- At least two near-silences before the turn / peak; sound as a transition (J/L-cut) at least twice.

## 7. Type
- Caption design is part of the style (font, position, how it enters). Keep it off the subject.
